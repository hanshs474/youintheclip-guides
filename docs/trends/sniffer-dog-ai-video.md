# Sniffer Dog Meme

The sniffer dog meme is a run of AI-made TikTok clips from @stitchthesnitchh: a spaniel in a black working vest weaves through a rave crowd, sits down at one person's feet and stares up, and that person starts to cry while the caption says "bro is so cooked". "Caught this raver in Ibiza…" (17 July 2026) has 64.4 million plays, and new catches, at the pub and at the airport, were still going up this week.

## How to make it

1. **Pick a catch, or keep the default.** The default keeps your own clothes and puts you in the festival crowd at night. Tap the tie-dye festival, the airport or the sofa for that exact scene.
2. **Add one photo, face sharp.** Front-facing with the face clear works best, because the end of the clip is a close-up of your face as it crumbles. Two people in the photo means two travellers at the airport.
3. **Want your dog in it? Same photo.** A photo of you with your dog, whole body in view, and your dog is the one that sits at your feet in the vest.
4. **Post it with the caption.** The clip comes back with crowd noise under it and no text. Type "bro is so cooked 😭" over it in TikTok or Reels and post.
