# Blocus Poursuite AI

Blocus Poursuite means blockade chase. Since late September French high-school students have been blocking their school gates with bins, and on TikTok the prank of the week is a fake clip of you at one: a selfie at the blockade, the riot police behind you start running, you run. Captions say it plainly: prank your parents with it. Here you pick a template and the runner is you.

## How to make it

1. **Pick a template, or keep the default.** The default keeps your own clothes and puts you on a smoky street with the bins across it. Tap the cat, the puppy or the kitten in the cardigan for that exact chase.
2. **Add one clear photo, face to the camera.** A selfie works best, because the clip is filmed like one. Good light on your face matters most when you look back at the police.
3. **Make it, then send it.** The clip comes back with shouting and a siren in it. Send it to the family chat with no caption, or add the trend sound on TikTok.
4. **Then put the dog in one.** The clip stays in your library. Run it again with your pet's photo and send the version where the dog is the one fleeing.
