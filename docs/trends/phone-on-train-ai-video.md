# Phone on Train Trend

The phone on train trend is the one where someone sticks their phone to the outside of a train window, sings to it from the platform while it films them, then runs after the train as it leaves with the phone. The caption is usually "Anyone seen a phone stuck to the train recently?", and the sound is often Dua Lipa's "Training Season". Here you post yours without a real train taking a real phone.

## How to make it

1. **Pick a platform, or keep your own look.** The default keeps your outfit and puts you on a subway platform. Tap the sprint, the tram, the Eiffel Tower ride or the hoodie walk for that exact take.
2. **Add one photo, head to toe if you have it.** Face clear and toward the camera. A full-length photo gives the sprint something to work from; a waist-up selfie works too. Two people in the photo means two of you on the tram take.
3. **Make it, then post it with your sound.** The clip comes back with a backing track. Put "Training Season" on it in TikTok or Reels, and the caption everyone uses, before you post.
