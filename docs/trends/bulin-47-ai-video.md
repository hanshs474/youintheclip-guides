# Bulin 47 AI Video

The Bulin 47 AI video trend puts someone new on the mic in one famous moment: Dominican artist Bulin 47 freestyling over a dembow beat outside a restaurant in Spain while Kevin Wai holds a portable speaker up to him. The AI version keeps the red-lit door, the crowd and the speaker, and the face is yours.

## How to make it

1. **Pick a template, or keep the default.** The default keeps your own clothes. Tap grandma for the dressed-up night-out look, the red stairs for a black tee and a watch, or the white tee for the chain.
2. **Add one clear photo, face to the camera.** Waist-up is enough. Good light on the face matters more than the background, which gets swapped for the red stairs anyway.
3. **Make it, then post it with the sound.** Your clip comes back with its own dembow beat. On TikTok or Reels, pick "Dame Hombro" before you post and it lands like the remakes on your For You page.
