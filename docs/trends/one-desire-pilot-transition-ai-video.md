# One Desire Pilot Transition

The One Desire pilot transition is a TikTok cut where you lip-sync in an ordinary room and, on the drop, the video jumps to you in an airliner cockpit, pilot cap on. The song is "One Desire", the electro house single by the French team Jakarta that charted in France in 2008; the cut took off in October 2026 from a creator's room-to-cockpit clip. Here the cockpit half is already built, and your photo is who sits in it.

## How to make it

1. **Pick a take, or keep the default.** The default keeps your own clothes for the room half and gives you a black captain's jacket, cap and aviators for the cockpit. Tap the bedroom, the hotel mirror or the coast road for that exact take.
2. **Add one photo with your face clear.** A front-facing selfie is enough. The room half is close-up, and the cap sits on whoever is in the photo.
3. **Make it, then add One Desire.** The clip comes back with an electro beat under it. In TikTok or Reels, put Jakarta's "One Desire" on it and post.

## What the takes look like

**The bedroom to the captain's seat.** Oversized tee, hair everywhere, singing to the phone. Then the drop, and the same face under a black pilot cap with the panels lit up behind.
