# Anthropomorphic Dog AI

The anthropomorphic dog trend turns one photo of your dog into a clip of that dog standing up on its back legs like a person and dancing toward the camera. On TikTok it is a CapCut AI template, and most posts this week are tutorials on finding it. This is the quickest way to your own: no install, no template link to hunt down, six takes already open, and the dog is whichever one is in your photo.

## How to make it

1. **Pick a take, or keep the default.** The default keeps your dog in the room from your photo and has it stand up and dance toward you. Tap the courtyard, the puppies, the car show, the kitchen, the beach or the hospital for that exact take.
2. **Add one clear photo of your dog.** Face sharp and the whole dog in the picture works best, so the legs it stands on look like its own. One dog per photo.
3. **Make it, then post it.** The clip comes back with a bouncy track under it. Swap in a trending sound in TikTok or Reels if you like, and post.
4. **Then do the next dog.** Your first clip waits in your library. Run the beach take with your other dog, or your friend's, and post them together.
