# Bad Times AI

The Bad Times AI trend is a short vertical clip made from one photo: a pet, or sometimes a toddler, stands up and argues along to Bad Times by Imael Angel, eyes closed, a paw raised like "hold on", a head tilt of disbelief and a lunge at the lens. Owners post their own dogs and cats doing it on TikTok, and the one photo you already have is all it takes to make yours.

## How to make it

1. **Pick a template, or keep your pet's own room.** The default keeps the room in your photo. Tap the shiba close-up, the red bar, the lawn or the velvet lounge to set the scene instead; the toddler template is there for a child's photo.
2. **Add one clear photo.** Face toward the camera, whole head in frame. A photo of your pet sitting up or standing gives the paws room to move.
3. **Make it, then add the sound.** Your Bad Times AI clip comes back with its own beat. In TikTok or Reels, pick the Bad Times sound before you post so the singing lands on the song everyone knows.
