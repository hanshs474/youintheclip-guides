# Billie Jean Dance AI

Billie Jean dance AI is the shortcut into the Billie Jean dance challenge: you upload one photo and get a short vertical clip of you doing the routine people are posting to the song, the side kicks, knee pops, lunge and point, spin and clap over the head. The challenge itself is real people dancing; the AI version puts you in it without learning a single count.

## How to make it

1. **Pick a spot, or keep your own look.** The default keeps your outfit and puts you in a bright dance studio. Tap the garage, the clifftop, the city wall or the white tee to dance there instead.
2. **Add one photo, head to toe if you have it.** Face clear and toward the camera. A full-length photo gives the kicks and the spin something to work from; a waist-up selfie works too.
3. **Make it, then post it with the song.** Your clip comes back with its own 80s beat. Add the Billie Jean sound in TikTok or Reels before you post.
