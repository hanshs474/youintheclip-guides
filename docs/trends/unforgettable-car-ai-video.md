# Unforgettable Car Trend AI

The Unforgettable car trend is a short vertical reveal set to French Montana and Swae Lee's "Unforgettable": you hold a 2 and a 4 up to the lens, they sweep past, and when the frame clears you are standing in front of a car. Owners film it with their own cars; the AI version starts from one photo and parks the car for you.

## How to make it

1. **Pick the car, or keep the default.** The default keeps your own clothes and puts a low coupe behind you at night. Tap the cream coupe, the black SUV or the garage roof for that exact scene.
2. **Add one clear photo, face to the camera.** Chest-up is enough. The cards cover your face for a second, so what matters is good light on it before and after.
3. **Make it, then post it with the song.** Your clip comes back with its own beat. Pick "Unforgettable" as the sound on TikTok or Reels and it lands like the reveals on your For You page.
