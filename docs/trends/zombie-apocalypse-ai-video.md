# Zombie AI Trend

The zombie AI trend is a short zombie apocalypse AI video about two people who love each other: one has turned, the other has a gun and can't use it, and the clip cuts to the life they had before. Couples, parents and kids, best friends and pet owners post it with their own faces on TikTok and Instagram. Here you pick a scene and it is your story.

## How to make it

1. **Pick the story, or keep the default.** The default is the classic: a ruined building, the gun, the drop, then a golden field in the mountains. Tap the mum, the kitchen, the meadow or the courtyard for that exact scene.
2. **Add one photo with both faces in it.** A couple selfie, you and your mum, you and your best friend, you and the dog. Faces clear and toward the camera, because the close-ups are where the tears land.
3. **Make it, then post it with the sound.** The clip comes back with soft piano in it. On TikTok or Reels you can swap in the sad trend sound before you post.
