# Babydoll Dance Video

The Babydoll dance video is the little retro two-step people do to "Babydoll", the single Jamie Miller released on 7 August 2026 on Republic Records: side by side, step left, step right, snap, shimmy the shoulders, kick, then arms out wide. Friends, dads with their daughters and whole dance-fitness classes have filmed it since. This is the shortest way to your own take: the steps are already here, and the dancer is whoever is in your photo.

## How to make it

1. **Pick a take, or keep the default.** The default keeps your own clothes and puts you in a bright studio. Tap the salon, the suits, the boutique or the living room for that exact take.
2. **Add one photo, head to toe if you have it.** A front-facing photo with the face sharp works. Full body is best, because the two-step is mostly feet and shoulders. Two people in the photo means two dancers.
3. **Make it, then add the sound.** The clip comes back with a soul beat under it. In TikTok or Reels, put Jamie Miller's "Babydoll" on it and post.
4. **Then do the rest of the crew.** Your first take is saved to your library. Run the suit take with your dad's photo next, or your whole friend group two at a time.
