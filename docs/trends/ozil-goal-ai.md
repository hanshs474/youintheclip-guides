# Ozil Goal AI

The Ozil goal AI trend remakes Mesut Özil's late winner for Arsenal away at Ludogorets in the Champions League on 1 November 2016: through on goal, he chipped the keeper, cut inside two defenders who slid past him, and slotted it in from close range. On TikTok people put themselves in that finish from one photo. Here you pick a pitch and the goal is yours.

## How to make it

1. **Pick the pitch, or keep the default.** The default keeps your own clothes and puts you on a floodlit pitch at night. Tap the school match, the sunset park or the hoodie kickabout for that exact scene.
2. **Add one clear photo, face to the camera.** Full body or waist-up both work. Good light on your face matters most, because the camera stays on you through the celebration.
3. **Make it, then post it.** The clip comes back with the crowd noise in it. Add the trend sound on TikTok or Reels if you want it to sit next to the Ozil edits on your For You page.
4. **Send one back with their face.** The clip stays in your library. Run it again with the photo of the friend who never passes, and post the goal they will never score.
