# Tecktonik Dance AI

The Tecktonik dance AI trend is a short vertical clip made from one photo: you stand on a raised planter behind the DJ booth at a night courtyard party, string lights overhead, and do the late-2000s electro arm dance while the crowd below films you. It started from a real party video that went viral in June, where one guy climbed up and brought Tecktonik back; the template rebuilds that night around whoever is in the photo.

## How to make it

1. **Pick a look, or keep your own.** The default prompt keeps the outfit in your photo. Tap the red jacket, the white shirt and tie, the dark jumpsuit or the wide crowd shot to dress the part instead.
2. **Add one photo, head to toe if you have it.** Face clear and toward the camera. A full-length photo gives the arms and legs something to work from; a waist-up selfie works too.
3. **Make it, then post it with the sound.** Your Tecktonik dance AI clip comes back with its own electro beat. On TikTok or Reels you can swap in the trend sound everyone is using before you post.
