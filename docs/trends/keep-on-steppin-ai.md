# Keep On Steppin AI

The Keep On Steppin AI trend is a short clip in which one photo becomes five people lined up in a parking lot, doing a stepping line dance in perfect sync. The dance and the song come first: "Keep On Steppin'" is a southern soul hit by Mike Clark Jr., and people line-dance to it for real. The AI version clones you into the whole line.

## How to make it

1. **Pick a look, or keep the default.** The default lines you up in five different suits. Tap the school jumpers for the low-angle version, or the night car park for the hoodie-and-beanie look.
2. **Add one clear photo, full length if you have it.** Face toward the camera. A head-to-toe photo gives the legs something to step with; a waist-up photo works too.
3. **Make it, then post it with the song.** Your clip comes back with its own beat. On TikTok or Reels, pick "Keep On Steppin'" before you post and it lands like the line-ups you have been seeing.
