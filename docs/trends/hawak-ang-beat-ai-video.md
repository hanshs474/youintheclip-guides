# Hawak Mo Ang Beat AI

Hawak Mo Ang Beat AI turns one photo of a person or pet into a clip of them doing the Filipino budots dance to "Hawak Mo ang Beat" (Tagalog for "you hold the beat"), with nobody learning or filming the routine. The real dance took over zumba classes and school energizers in early 2026; the AI clips came back this October.

## How to make it

1. **Pick a place, or keep the default.** The default keeps you right where your photo was taken. Tap the park, the kitchen, the beach or the night street to dance there instead.
2. **Add one clear photo, full body if you have it.** The routine has a side lunge and a fist above the head, so a photo from the knees up gives it the most to work with. Face sharp, no sunglasses unless you want them in the clip.
3. **Make it, then add the song.** The clip comes back with a budots beat in it. Put the Hawak Mo Ang Beat sound on in TikTok or Reels before you post.
4. **Then do the whole family.** Your first one is saved to your history. Run the same dance again with your mum's photo, your barkada's, the dog's, and post them together.
