# Toz Pembe Dance AI

Toz Pembe ("powder pink") is the summer 2026 single by manifest, the Turkish girl group. On Instagram people film a before and after for it: an ordinary outfit, then a cut to full pink and the dance. The AI version took off on TikTok in October, when @caotic.avocado posted three cats in wigs and pink sequins dancing it in sync at a giant boombox. Here, your face takes the front spot.

## How to make it

1. **Keep the boombox stage, or pick a glow-up.** The default puts you front and centre in pink sequins with two backup dancers. Tap a template for the living-room, doorway or close-up glow-up instead.
2. **Add one photo, face clear, body in view if you can.** Waist up or full body works best, since the dance is filmed wide. For a pet, one animal with its face and markings visible.
3. **Make it, then add the Toz Pembe sound.** The clip comes back on a pop beat. Pick the Toz Pembe sound in TikTok or Reels and the moves land on the song.
4. **Then do the whole group chat.** Run it again with your best friend's photo, or the cat's, and post the set.
