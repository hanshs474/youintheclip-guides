# Nicole Kidman Dance AI

The Nicole Kidman dance is the TikTok routine people do to "Nicole Kidman", the ADÉLA song whose hook is "I feel like Nicole". It is not a scene from one of her films. Covers were on r/Dance by mid-September; in October it is all over TikTok and Reels: hands to the chest, a point at the lens, hip sways, a kick, one arm up, a turn. Here the steps come with the template and the dancer is you.

## How to make it

1. **Pick a take, or keep the default.** The default keeps your own clothes and puts you on a sunny street. Tap the graffiti wall, Paris, the studio, the living room or the garage for that exact take.
2. **Add one clear photo, full body if you have it.** A front-facing photo with your face sharp works. Head to toe is best, because the dance is mostly legs and hips.
3. **Make it, then add the sound.** The clip comes back with a beat under it. In TikTok or Reels, put the ADÉLA "Nicole Kidman" sound on it and post.
4. **Then do your friends.** The clip stays in your library. Run the trio template with your best friend's photo next, and post both.
