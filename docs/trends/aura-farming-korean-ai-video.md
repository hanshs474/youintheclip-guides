# Aura Farming Korean AI

Aura farming Korean AI is a short K-drama classroom clip made from one photo: students dancing on the desks, the teacher glaring from the green board, and the main character perfectly calm under red and blue neon, dark sunglasses on. Aura farming is the slang for doing exactly that on purpose: looking effortlessly cool on camera. YouTube edit tutorials tag the trend after Park Yoon-ho and Study Group.

## How to make it

1. **Pick a role, or keep the default.** The default is the transfer-student entrance. Tap the window ledge or the teacher at the green board to play that part instead.
2. **Add one sharp photo of your face.** A sharp, front-facing photo works best; the clip ends close on your face. Waist-up is plenty, and no sunglasses in the photo: the template adds them.
3. **Make it, then add the trend sound.** The clip comes back with its own moody beat. Pick the aura farming sound in Instagram or TikTok when you post.
4. **Then cast your friends.** Your first clip waits in your library. Run the teacher template with a friend's photo and post the two side by side.
