# Rumpelstiltskin AI

The Rumpelstiltskin AI trend is a short clip of a gnome-like imp from the Brothers Grimm fairy tale, tip-toeing through a hay-filled stable with a smug, mischievous grin. The original is AI-generated, first posted on 27 August by the Instagram account stroinaya, and it usually runs to Riff Raff's "tip toeing in my jordans". This template rebuilds the barn and the costume around whoever is in your photo.

## How to make it

1. **Pick a look, or keep the default.** The default dresses you in the bowl cut and moustache in the hay barn. Tap the barn tip-toe to keep your own face and hair, or the blue-backdrop imp for the curly-haired turn and grin.
2. **Add one clear photo.** Face toward the camera, good light. A waist-up photo is plenty; the costume goes on top of it.
3. **Make it, then post it with the sound.** Your clip comes back with its own playful beat. On TikTok or Reels, pick "tip toeing in my jordans" before you post and it lands like the clips you have been seeing.
4. **Send one back with their face.** The clip stays in your library. Run it again with a friend's photo and reply to the next imp meme they send you.
