# Happy Friday I'm Onnat AI

A two-beat football joke: a serious game-day stare for "Happy Friday", then the same player squeezed into a toddler's ride-on car for "I'm onnat". The line "I'm onnat, good morning" went round TikTok in the second week of October, and by 12 October football had put a toy car under it. Most posts since are template tutorials. This is the shortest way to the clip: the template is already here, and the player is whoever is in your photo.

## How to make it

1. **Pick a car, or keep the default.** The default puts you in a red jersey in the red-and-yellow coupe under the lights. Tap the jeep or the pink convertible for that exact ride.
2. **Add one clear photo, face to the camera.** Any photo where the face is sharp works, in uniform or not. The clip opens on a long stare into the lens, so that face carries the joke.
3. **Make it, then post it with the sound.** The clip comes back with crowd noise in it. Add the Happy Friday sound and the two captions in TikTok or Reels.
4. **Then do the whole roster.** The clip stays in your library. Run it again with the next starter's photo, or the dog's, and post them one after another.
